# qlik-answers-executive-brief

A tone skill for Qlik Answers. Every answer starts with the answer and the number, adds up to three one-line bullets, stays under 60 words and uses no emoji. Charts and tables still appear.

**Example**

User: "What were total sales last year?"

Answer: "Sales last year: [amount], [pct] above the year before." followed by up to three short bullets.

User: "Which region should I cut?"

Answer: "The data can't make that call. Lowest sales: [region], [amount], [pct] below the average."

**Tested:** median answer of 36 to 46 words, against about 100 without the skill. Charts were kept and no emoji appeared.
